## 🌊 Overview

Love the thrill of adding items to your cart, but hate the sight of your bank account emptying? **FauxCart** (also known as Dopamine SafeShop) is your therapeutic solution.

Experience the dopamine hit of a real eCommerce platform — browse products, add them to your cart, and securely check out — with **zero actual cost**. No real packages will arrive, and no money will be deducted. It's safe, guilt-free retail therapy!

## 💎 Key Features

- 🎒 **Endless Browsing:** Explore a wide catalog of products populated seamlessly via the *Fake Store API*.
- 🛍️ **Simulated Cart:** Add, remove, and adjust quantities of products in your Impulse Cart.
- 💳 **Guilt-Free Checkout:** Hit the "Complete Virtual Purchase" button and enjoy a satisfaction rush as your total "spent" is zeroed out without touching your real wallet.
- ✨ **Fluid Animations:** Smooth drawer transitions and micro-interactions powered by `motion` keep the experience premium and satisfying.
- 🎨 **Professional Polish Theme:** A slick, blue-tinted modern UI designed to look exactly like high-end eCommerce.

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Framework:** [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, React 19)
- **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Animations:** [Motion](https://motion.dev/)
- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Data Source:** [Fake Store API](https://fakestoreapi.com/)

## 🛠️ Getting Started

1. **Clone the repository.**
2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```
3. **Run the development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
4. **Open your browser** and visit `http://localhost:3000` to start your guilt-free shopping spree!

## 📘 Disclaimer
This is a functional visual simulation. No real money can or will be processed. Enjoy your peace of mind as the true delivery.
